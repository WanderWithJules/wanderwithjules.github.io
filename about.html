About

Hi, I'm Julie 👋

Welcome to Wander With Jules.

I’m based in Dubai and love discovering places that make you stop and wonder, whether that’s a fascinating museum, a scenic road trip, a hidden café or somewhere completely unexpected.

Most of my adventures start with the same thought: Why not?

Some take weeks to plan. Others begin with a random decision and a full tank of fuel. Either way, they are usually worth the drive.

Why This Blog?

I started Wander With Jules to share honest experiences from the places I visit, along with practical tips, original photos and the little details that do not always make it into travel guides.

If I recommend somewhere, it is because I have actually been there. No copy-and-paste itineraries, just real experiences and honest opinions.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Museums and cultural attractions
  • Road trips around the UAE and beyond
  • Hidden gems worth discovering
  • Honest reviews and practical travel tips
  • Plenty of spontaneous adventures

Thanks for Stopping By

Whether you are planning your next trip or simply looking for somewhere new to explore, I hope you find something here that inspires you.

See you on the road.
